# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

This project is named for a memory style — *Independent Autistic Intelligence* —
and neurodivergent contributors are explicitly welcome here. Directness is not
rudeness, a request for literal and specific wording is not obstruction, and
depth of focus on one thread is not a failure to engage. Assume good faith in
both directions.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — on code, on prose,
  on benchmark numbers.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves.
- Saying plainly what you mean, and asking plainly when you do not understand.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information — a physical or email address, store
  contents, log excerpts containing someone's captured memory — without their
  explicit permission.
- Mocking or dismissing someone for how they communicate, including for being
  blunt, verbose, literal, or slow to reply.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and
will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned
with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions — and also when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the
maintainers, through the team at [lcgc.dev](https://lcgc.dev) or via GitHub's
private reporting on this repository. Do not report a conduct issue in a public
issue thread.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Reporters'
privacy and security will be respected.

### Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction** — for unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour. A private,
   written warning with clarity about the violation, and possibly a request for
   a public apology.
2. **Warning** — for a violation through a single incident or series of
   actions. A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no
   interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — for a serious violation, including sustained
   inappropriate behaviour. A temporary ban from any interaction with the
   project.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violation, harassment of an individual,
   or aggression toward classes of individuals. A permanent ban from any public
   interaction with the project.
